## Included evidence

- Frozen six-model validation/test summaries and associated metric/latency records.
- Selected exact-device and physical-workflow analyses.
- Unmodified host-resource JSONL samples and complete-service summary from the retained stationary run.
- Loaded-network sample CSV and summary.
- Accepted simulated-mission event log, manifest, contact sheet and continuous recording.
- Dataset-freeze hashes, selected lineage manifests and dated reports.

The original failed attempts, full device snapshots, training outputs and complete raw archives remain retained locally. Their absence from this curated repository is an availability limitation, not evidence that the attempts succeeded. Hashes identify retained evidence but do not make unavailable inputs reconstructible.

Reproduction requiring these inputs needs separate acquisition, matching hashes and permission checks. The original dataset provenance is described in [the dataset report](docs/M3_CURATED_RESEARCH_DATASET_20260729.md) and the thesis.

No FASDD-UAV or MmodalFire predictive evaluation result is claimed. Their preparation/admission configuration remains useful for future work but does not indicate completed external validation.

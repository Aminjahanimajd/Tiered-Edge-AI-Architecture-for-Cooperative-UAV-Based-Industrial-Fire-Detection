\chapter{Experiment and Claim Register}
\label{app:evidence}

This appendix connects the thesis narrative to the authoritative experiment
register and the evidence accepted at the 30 September cutoff. ``Completed with
limitation'' means that usable evidence exists but its sample, duration or
conditions restrict the claim. ``Historical proof-of-function'' denotes
implementation evidence that was not collected under a controlled quantitative
protocol. ``Not executed'' and ``not evaluated'' identify registered work that
contributes no positive result to the final evidence set.

\section{Research-question closure}

The final evidence supports bounded answers to RQ1--RQ4. RQ1 identifies
different internal predictive and runtime trade-offs and demonstrates the
historical deployed pair, but it does not provide a complete candidate-by-device
ranking. RQ2 establishes more than 30 minutes of stationary concurrent-service
operation, while retaining the failed Scout latency and strict Sentinel
swap-growth criteria. RQ3 establishes correlated, condition-sensitive Scout
verdicts and their decision latency, but not the effect size of the temporal
filter or tiered workflow. RQ4 establishes executable nominal and selected-fault
development paths in simulation, but not a mission-reliability estimate. RQ5
was not evaluated because neither external source reached a frozen eligible
inference manifest. These dispositions are used consistently in the abstract,
results, discussion and conclusion.
